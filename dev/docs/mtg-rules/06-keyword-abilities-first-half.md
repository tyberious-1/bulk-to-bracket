# CR 702 keyword abilities, part 1 (702.1 - 702.90), notes for a card-reading engine

Type key: S = static, T = triggered, A = activated, R = replacement/prevention, C = cost-modifier/alt/additional cost, CDA = characteristic-defining.
Note: prowess is NOT in this chunk (it is 702.108, later). Redundancy: nearly every evasion/static keyword says "multiple instances redundant"; repeated copies of a triggered keyword (rampage, flanking, bushido, cascade, storm, annihilator...) trigger separately.

## General rules (702.1)
- 702.1a: "[keyword] cost" means only the variable part (scavenge cost = mana cost, but the ability's activation cost also includes exiling the card).
- 702.1b: a granted keyword with a variable (echo {X} where X = life) is re-evaluated constantly (Past in Flames gives flashback = mana cost; a split card uses the half cast).
- 702.1c: "the same is true for [keywords]" grants every variant of landwalk/protection among the creatures.
- 702.1d: "with flashback" = "with a flashback ability". A reader keyed on keyword text should also find granted keywords (Varolz, Past in Flames, Snapcaster-style effects).

## Combat keywords
- **Deathtouch 702.2 (S)**: any nonzero damage from the source destroys a creature (SBA). Any nonzero combat damage counts as lethal when assigning, so a deathtouch+trample attacker assigns 1 to each blocker and tramples the rest. Works from any zone; uses last-known info. Needs: damage-source doublers, fight/bite effects (any damage, not just combat) all become removal. Toughness-0 creatures are not destroyed by it. Indestructible ignores it.
- **Defender 702.3 (S)**: can't attack. Still blocks and can tap for abilities. Cards that "assign damage equal to toughness" or give it away (Wall payoffs) are the enablers; a defender commander is a normal body, not a dead card.
- **Double strike 702.4 (S)**: deals damage in both the first-strike and the regular step. Two combat damage steps happen if any attacker or blocker has first strike or double strike when the damage step begins. Traps: (1) each hit is a separate damage event, so lifelink, infect, deathtouch, "deals combat damage to a player" triggers, and commander damage all happen twice; (2) giving double strike after the first step still lets it deal damage in the second step; (3) removing it during the first step stops the second hit; (4) +1 power pumps and damage doublers scale at 2x.
- **First strike 702.7 (S)**: deals damage in the first step only (the regular step is skipped for it). A creature given first strike after the first step has passed still deals regular damage; one that loses it after dealing first-strike damage does not deal damage again (unless double strike). Interaction: first strike + deathtouch kills a blocker before it deals damage; it only matters if something has first strike at the start of the step. A creature without it that survives still deals damage in the second step.
- **Flying 702.9 / Reach 702.17 (S)**: flying attackers can be blocked only by flying or reach creatures. A flier can block anything. Reach is not flying: it does not evade. Flying is a mutual-removal check for "creatures without flying" wraths/hate effects. Landwalk, flying, fear, shadow etc. are all "evasion" for anything keyed to "evasion".
- **Haste 702.10 (S)**: attack and use {T}/{Q} abilities the turn it comes in. Needed for mana creatures with {T} costs (gives it to them) and for ETB-then-attack plans. Granted haste is not "has haste" before it is granted.
- **Intimidate 702.13 / Fear 702.36 (S)**: blocked only by artifact creatures and (intimidate) creatures sharing a color / (fear) black creatures. Colorless non-artifact creatures cannot block intimidate attackers.
- **Landwalk 702.14 (S)**: unblockable if the defending player controls a land of that type; not "cancelled" by your own matching land. Includes nonbasic, snow, legendary and "artifact landwalk" forms. Commander tables with Islands = islandwalk is live; Eluge-style "lands become Islands" effects enable it.
- **Shadow 702.28 (S)**: shadow creatures block and are blocked only by shadow creatures. Symmetric: a shadow creature cannot block normal attackers.
- **Horsemanship 702.31 (S)**: can't be blocked by non-horsemanship creatures; can block anything. (Mostly unavailable in Commander.)
- **Trample 702.19 (S)**: assign lethal damage to all blockers, remainder to the player/planeswalker/battle. Lethal accounts for damage already marked and other attackers' damage. With deathtouch, 1 per blocker is lethal. If the blocker is removed, all damage goes through. "Trample over planeswalkers" (702.19c) also lets excess go to the planeswalker's controller after loyalty. A tramplers vs a protection blocker still must assign lethal (prevented) damage first. A trampling attacker blocked by nothing assigns everything to the player (702.19d). Without trample over planeswalkers, a creature attacking a planeswalker can't hit the player. Trample does nothing on a blocker or for noncombat damage.
- **Vigilance 702.20 (S)**: attacking doesn't tap it. Enables attacking and still using {T} abilities or blocking; enabler for "tap an untapped creature you control" costs and convoke after attacking.
- **Banding 702.22 (S)**: attackers band (one non-banding creature may join). If one in the band is blocked all are; the defender assigns damage of a creature blocked by a banding creature, the attacker assigns damage of a blocker blocking a band. Rare; "bands with other" is the quality variant.
- **Rampage 702.23 (T)**: when blocked, +N/+N for each blocker beyond the first; computed once on resolution.
- **Flanking 702.25 (T)**: when blocked by a creature without flanking, the blocker gets -1/-1.
- **Bushido 702.45 (T)**: +N/+N when it blocks or becomes blocked.
- **Frenzy 702.68 (T)**: when it attacks and isn't blocked, +N/+0.
- **Provoke 702.39 (T)**: on attack, target creature the defender controls must block it if able, and untaps.
- **Exalted 702.83 (T)**: whenever a creature you control attacks alone, that creature gets +1/+1. Each instance on any permanent you control triggers; it counts even when exalted is on a different permanent. "Attacks alone" = only declared attacker (so token makers that add attackers turn it off).
- **Annihilator 702.86 (T)**: when it attacks, defending player sacrifices N permanents. Needs to attack and survive to attack; "defending player" is the one attacked, not all opponents.
- **Poisonous 702.70 (T)**: combat damage to a player gives N poison counters (per instance, per damage event); a poison plan pairs it with double strike and proliferate.
- **Infect 702.90 (S, R)**: damage to a player becomes poison counters (no life loss); damage to a creature becomes -1/-1 counters. Not "combat damage only" - any infect-source damage. The damage is still damage, so lifelink still gains life. Pairs with proliferate, persist/undying (persist checks -1/-1 counters), wither.
- **Wither 702.80 (S, R)**: damage to creatures is -1/-1 counters instead of marked damage; they stay (damage does not heal). Persist's gate (no -1/-1 counters) is turned off by wither/infect damage.
- **Absorb 702.64 (S, R)**: prevents N of damage from each source each time. Partial prevention of Commander-style attackers.

## Protection, ward, hexproof, shroud, indestructible
- **Protection 702.16 (S)**: DEBT: Damage, Enchant/Equip, Block, Target (all from a stated quality). Quality can be a color, type, subtype, player, "everything", or a card name only if specified as a name. Aura/equipment of the quality fall off (SBA); damage is prevented; can't be blocked by creatures with the quality. Traps: it stops targeting but NOT non-targeted mass effects (wrath, "each creature" damage), and doesn't stop sacrifice or non-targeted control changes. Protection from a card type/subtype also covers non-battlefield sources of that type. Protection from everything cannot be targeted by anything, blocked, equipped or enchanted; "protection from [player]" covers everything that player controls/owns. A creature with protection from its own color can't be equipped by own-color equipment. Trample vs a protection-from-attacker blocker must still assign lethal damage to the blocker.
- **Hexproof 702.11 (S)**: can't be targeted by opponents' spells/abilities. Your own can target it. "Hexproof from [quality]" is a variant: losing hexproof removes all of them; "as though it didn't have hexproof" effects also beat the variant. Doesn't stop non-targeting effects.
- **Shroud 702.18 (S)**: no one can target it, including its controller. Blocks your own equip/aura/pump: a shroud creature can't be equipped or enchanted by you (enchant targets).
- **Ward 702.21 (T)**: when it becomes the target of an opponent's spell or ability, counter that spell/ability unless that player pays the cost. Taxes only targeting by opponents; it is a trigger, so it can be responded to, Stifled, and does not stop non-targeting or the controller's own spells. X in ward cost is determined on resolution. Ward is a tax on spells/abilities that target, including activated abilities. Gets counted per copy of ward (each triggers). Cheap ward = soft; "pay life", "discard", "sacrifice" ward is a real cost.
- **Indestructible 702.12 (S)**: can't be destroyed (lethal damage, deathtouch, "destroy" effects). Does not stop exile, sacrifice, -X/-X toughness 0, legend rule, state-based deaths other than destroy. A creature with toughness 0 still dies.
- **Umbra armor 702.89 (S, R)**: on an Aura; if the enchanted permanent would be destroyed, remove damage and destroy the Aura instead. Needs the Aura to stay attached (protection or hexproof can't stop an Aura already attached).
- **Phasing 702.26 (S)**: phases out in its controller's untap step, phases back in the next. While out it doesn't exist (isn't affected by wraths, can't be targeted, doesn't count for devotion or "for each creature you control", doesn't trigger zone-change abilities, leaves combat). Attached Auras/Equipment phase out with it. Tokens survive. Phase-in doesn't trigger ETB; counters stay.

## Cost-reduction / alternative / additional costs
- **Affinity 702.41 (C, S on stack)**: costs {1} less per [thing] you control; applies after cost determination, reduces only generic mana. Affinity for artifacts wants cheap artifacts (treasures, clues, thopters); count it at cast time.
- **Convoke 702.51 (C)**: tap untapped creatures you control to pay one mana each: colored creature pays that color or generic; colorless/other-color creature pays generic. Not an alternative/additional cost; applies after reductions and is limited to the total cost. Tapped creatures can't attack/use {T}; "tap" effects trigger "tapped creature" payoffs. Tokens and summoning-sick creatures can convoke.
- **Delve 702.66 (C)**: exile a card from your graveyard for each generic mana. Not a cost reduction in the "less to cast" sense (so reducers apply first). Competes with flashback/escape/graveyard payoffs for the same cards; feeds on self-mill, discard, spells in the yard; hurts Persist/recursion plans.
- **Kicker 702.33 (C)**: optional additional cost; kicked spell/permanent has bonus text ("if kicked"). Multikicker pays repeatedly. Kicker 2+ costs total mana value stays unchanged, kicker costs don't change mana value. Needs spare mana; counts as "cast" for cast triggers and as an "additional cost" for cost reducers. Sticker kicker adds a ticket counter.
- **Buyback 702.27 (C)**: additional cost; if paid, the spell returns to hand instead of graveyard. Wants mana, cost reduction, and spell-copy or untap/cast engines; a spell exiled by Rebound/Flashback can't be bought back.
- **Entwine 702.42 (C)**: pay extra to choose all modes (resolved in printed order).
- **Replicate 702.56 (C+T)**: pay replicate cost N times as additional cost, then copy the spell N times (trigger on cast). Copies are not cast, so no cast triggers.
- **Conspire 702.78 (C+T)**: tap two untapped creatures sharing a color with the spell as an additional cost; copy on cast. Convoke/vigilance enablers apply.
- **Offering 702.48 (C)**: sacrifice a permanent of a type; spell cost is reduced by that permanent's mana cost and it gains flash. Creature-sac/ETB value shells.
- **Splice 702.47 (C)**: reveal from hand, pay cost, its text is added to the spell; remains in hand.
- **Prowl 702.76 (C)**: alternate cost if you dealt combat damage this turn with a source sharing a creature type with the spell. Needs combat damage from a creature of that type.
- **Evoke 702.74 (C+T)**: cast for the evoke cost; sacrificed on entering (the ETB still happens, the sacrifice trigger is separate and can be stacked). Pairs with blink and sacrifice outlets to get both ETB and a body, and with "whenever a creature enters/dies" triggers.
- **Morph/Megamorph 702.37 (C)**: cast face down for {3} as a 2/2 with no name/types; turn up as a special action for the morph cost (not on the stack, can't be responded to). ETB abilities do not trigger on turning face-up. Megamorph adds a +1/+1 counter. Face-down creatures are colorless; "enters" triggers fire on the face-down 2/2 and see no text.

## Spell-copy / cast triggers
- **Storm 702.40 (T on stack)**: copies for each spell cast before it this turn (any player, including countered and your own); the copies aren't cast (no cast triggers). Storm count includes spells that were countered. Reduces to: cheap spells first, then the storm spell. Mana rituals, cost reducers, free spells, and "cast" triggers all increase it.
- **Gravestorm 702.69 (T)**: copy for each permanent that went from the battlefield to a graveyard this turn (tokens count).
- **Cascade 702.85 (T)**: on cast, exile from the top until a nonland card with a lower mana value; you may cast it free, then the rest go to the bottom in a random order. Reads the spell's mana value (so X spells count as X = 0 on the stack; MV is mana cost, not what you paid); it is a cast, so it triggers "whenever you cast" and counters can hit it; cascade into a card with MV less than the spell. Multiple instances each trigger. A cascade spell can't hit lands or MV-equal cards. Reducing/modifying MV (e.g. copy effects, splice) matters. Free-cast spells with X use X = 0.
- **Ripple 702.60 (T)**: on cast reveal top N, free-cast any same-named cards.
- **Rebound 702.88 (S)**: if cast from hand, exile on resolution and cast free next upkeep. Not from graveyard/flashback/exile casts.
- **Epic 702.50 (S/delayed T)**: for the rest of the game you can't cast spells and copy the spell each upkeep. Effects can still put copies on the stack.
- **Split second 702.61 (S)**: while it is on the stack no one can cast spells or activate non-mana abilities; triggers still go on the stack, special actions and mana abilities still work.

## Graveyard/exile/zone-based
- **Flashback 702.34 (S)**: cast an instant/sorcery from the graveyard for the flashback cost; exiled when it would leave the stack. It uses an alternative cost, so can't combine with other alternatives (but can use additional costs and reducers). Flashback is a cast: it triggers storm and "whenever you cast". Past in Flames gives it to all.
- **Retrace 702.81 (S)**: cast from graveyard by discarding a land as an additional cost (normal mana cost, not exiled afterward). Land discard synergy; it goes back to the graveyard.
- **Cycling 702.29 (A from hand)**: pay and discard: draw a card. Is a discard (triggers "whenever you discard" and "cycle" triggers; cycle-or-discard triggers only once). Typecycling searches for a type instead of drawing (a cycling cost, not a draw). A card with cycling keeps the ability in all zones for "has an activated ability" checks. Feeds graveyard and madness.
- **Madness 702.35 (S+T)**: if discarded, exile it and may cast for the madness cost (trigger on the stack). If you don't cast it, it goes to the graveyard. Needs a discard outlet (cycling, looters, wheel effects).
- **Dredge 702.52 (R)**: instead of drawing, mill N and return the card; needs at least N cards in library. Replacement effect; replaces draws (so draw triggers still happen only if a draw happens; dredge means it doesn't). Compatible with self-mill and loot; it is a trap for decking.
- **Unearth 702.84 (A from graveyard)**: return to the battlefield, haste, exiled at the next end step or if it would leave. Sorcery-speed. ETB and attack triggers (blink, sac outlets, "creature entered" payoffs); exile replacement stops dies-triggers after the first return.
- **Recover 702.59 (T from graveyard)**: when a creature goes to your graveyard from the battlefield, pay or exile.
- **Haunt 702.55 (T)**: when it dies/resolves, exile it haunting a creature; triggers again when that creature dies.
- **Soulshift 702.46 (T)**: on dying, return a Spirit card with MV N or less.
- **Modular 702.43 (S+T)**: enters with N +1/+1 counters; when it dies, move counters to target artifact creature. Counts as +1/+1 counter synergy.
- **Persist 702.79 (T)**: when it dies, if it had no -1/-1 counters, return it with a -1/-1 counter. Gate is the -1/-1 counter: a card with the counter, or wither/infect damage, turns it off; it returns as a new object (ETB retriggers, a sacrifice outlet repeats once). Proliferate on its -1/-1 counter blocks it; removing the counter re-enables it.
- **Transmute 702.53 (A from hand, sorcery speed)**: discard, fetch a card with the same mana value. Tutor that needs matching MV.
- **Transfigure 702.71 (A)**: sacrifice, fetch a creature with the same MV onto the battlefield.
- **Reinforce 702.77 (A from hand)**: discard to put N +1/+1 counters on a target creature (instant speed).
- **Forecast 702.57 (A from hand)**: once per turn, only on your upkeep, reveal from hand.
- **Suspend 702.62 (S+T)**: exile with time counters for the suspend cost; free cast with haste (creatures) when the last counter is removed. Does not use the stack to exile. It is a cast (free), so cast triggers fire; it avoids counterspell timing at the cost of delay. Time counters can be added/removed by effects (Jhoira, Rift Elemental, Clockspinning).
- **Vanishing 702.63 / Fading 702.32 / Cumulative upkeep 702.24 / Echo 702.30 (T upkeep, self-limiting)**: sacrifice downsides. Vanishing and fading use time/fade counters and sacrifice at 0 (vanishing: on the last-counter-removed trigger; fading: when you can't remove one). Cumulative upkeep adds an age counter each upkeep and demands a cost that scales with counters (partial payment not allowed). Echo: pay at your next upkeep after it entered, else sacrifice. These upkeep taxes are drawbacks, so cards with them are cheap for their effect and strong with blink (resets echo/cumulative age counters).
- **Hideaway 702.75 (T)**: when it enters, look at top N, exile one face down, rest to the bottom randomly; the permanent later has a condition to play it free. The exiled card is playable by the permanent's own ability.
- **Champion 702.72 (T x2)**: ETB sacrifice unless you exile another object you control; the exiled card returns when champion leaves. Blink or removal of the champion returns the card; a lord-tribe cost.
- **Amplify 702.38 (S)**: enters with N counters per creature card of a shared type revealed (cards stay in hand). Type density matters.
- **Devour 702.82 (S)**: sacrifice any number of creatures on entering for N counters each; "it devoured" counts creatures. Pairs with token makers and sacrifice payoffs (the devoured creatures die, triggering death payoffs).
- **Graft 702.58 (S+T)**: enters with N counters; moves them to creatures that enter later.
- **Sunburst 702.44 (S)**: counters equal to colors of mana spent to cast; not alternative-cost-free (nothing spent means no counters). Multicolor ramp and Treasure/Eye-type mana matter.
- **Bloodthirst 702.54 (S)**: enters with N +1/+1 counters if an opponent was dealt damage this turn; X-variant counts total damage.
- **Level up 702.87 (A, sorcery speed)**: put level counters; not the same as Class levels.
- **Changeling 702.73 (CDA)**: is every creature type everywhere (also in hand, library, graveyard). Counts for every tribal lord/payoff; relevant to a tribal deck builder: count as a member of any tribe.
- **Aura swap 702.65 (A)**: exchange with an Aura in hand.
- **Enchant 702.5 (S)**: restricts the Aura's targets and what it can stay attached to; multiple enchant restrictions all apply. Enchant player Auras can't attach to permanents.
- **Equip 702.6 (A, sorcery speed)**: attach to a target creature you control (target, so hexproof/shroud/protection on your own creature can block you from equipping it, except you control hexproof). Equipment stays when the creature leaves. "Equip planeswalker" variant. Equip restrictions (equip legendary) restrict only the activation target, not what it may stay attached to. Multiple equip abilities may be used.
- **Fortify 702.67 (A)**: like equip for lands.
- **Ninjutsu 702.49 (A from hand)**: pay, reveal, return an unblocked attacker you control to hand; the ninja enters tapped and attacking the same target. It doesn't "declare" as an attacker (no attack triggers), but ETB triggers; return-to-hand re-enables ETB or "enters" triggers of the returned creature (value with commander damage/combat damage triggers). Commander ninjutsu 702.49d also works from the command zone and avoids commander tax.

## Traps summary
- Deathtouch + trample: 1 damage per blocker is lethal. Double strike doubles lifelink/infect/poison/commander damage events.
- Ward taxes only opponents' targeting; protection/hexproof/shroud stop only targeting (protection also damage/blocking/attaching), none stops wraths or sacrifice effects.
- Storm counts spells cast, cascade reads mana value of the spell and casts (triggers cast payoffs); storm/replicate/conspire copies are not cast.
- Convoke/delve/affinity apply after other cost modification; convoke can pay colored mana only with that color's creature, delve only generic.
- Persist is turned off by -1/-1 counters (wither, infect); evoke sacrifice is a trigger you can respond to.
- Many keywords function from non-battlefield zones (cycling/ninjutsu/forecast/reinforce/transmute in hand; flashback/retrace/unearth/dredge/recover in graveyard; suspend/rebound/hideaway in exile). A card-reading engine must note the zone.
